Data Protection

Privacy & Data Governance Policy

1. Scope and Principles

HeartSound AI values your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when using this research and screening-support application.

2. Audio Data & Storage Isolation

Uploaded and microphone-recorded heart sounds are saved under segregated, randomized UUID directory paths inaccessible through open web directory browsing. Audio files are processed solely on the backend to generate feature vectors, waveforms, spectrograms, and classification scores.

Audio files are NEVER transmitted to third-party language model providers or external data aggregators. Only sanitized text prompts within the AI Chatbot module are processed by configured LLMs.

3. Account & Demographic Data

We store only essential authentication credentials (encrypted via Django's PBKDF2 with SHA-256 password hasher) and optional health assessment questionnaire responses associated with your user ID.

4. Right to Deletion (Data Retention)

You maintain continuous control over your records. You can delete any individual heart sound recording and its associated waveform visuals and prediction results at any time via your user dashboard. Wiping an entry permanently removes the file from filesystem disk storage.

5. Audit Logging

For system integrity, non-medical operational audit entries (such as login timestamps, account creation, and analysis job executions) are stored in administrative logs with stripped query content.
