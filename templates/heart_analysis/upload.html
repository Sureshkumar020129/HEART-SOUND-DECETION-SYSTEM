Tips for Best Results
  • Record in a quiet environment to minimise background noise.
  • Use a good-quality stethoscope with a digital adapter, or place the microphone close to the chest.
  • Record at least 5–10 seconds of audio covering S1 and S2 heart sounds.
  • WAV format at 22 050 Hz or higher gives the best analysis quality.
  • This tool is for research/educational purposes — not clinical diagnosis.
