Project Background

About HeartSound AI

Project Overview

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) represents one of the leading causes of global mortality. While early clinical auscultation with a stethoscope is foundational, acoustic interpretation requires extensive clinical training, and subtle murmurs or diastolic anomalies can often be overlooked in early outpatient screenings.

HeartSound AI is a full-stack automated screening support and research application. By marrying digital phonocardiogram (PCG) acoustic processing, feature extraction (MFCCs, spectral contrast, chroma, mel-energies), and machine learning classification (Random Forest with class-balanced weighting), the system facilitates rapid preliminary triage and patient education.

Core Research Objectives

Signal Preprocessing Rigor

Consistent resampling to 22.05 kHz, mono downmixing, zero-padding/truncation to fixed time windows, and peak amplitude normalization.

Multi-Domain Audio Features

Integration of 40-order MFCC statistics, spectral centroid, spectral bandwidth, zero-crossing rates, and mel-filterbank energies.

Transparent AI Model Tiering

Clear distinction between algorithmic model confidence probabilities and clinical disease likelihood, preventing overconfidence.

Fail-Safe Interactive Chatbot

Configurable multi-provider LLM backend with emergency keyword detection that triggers immediate urgent triage instructions.

Academic & Research Standards

The machine learning pipeline is designed around datasets such as the PhysioNet / Computing in Cardiology Challenge 2016 Heart Sound Database. Data is partitioned with strict stratification and patient-level boundaries to prevent data leakage between training, validation, and test splits.

Institutional Medical Notice: HeartSound AI is engineered exclusively for academic, engineering demonstration, and research exploration. It does not possess CE mark or FDA clearance as a diagnostic medical device.
